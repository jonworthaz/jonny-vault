❋ Breathe & Chill
Take a moment to slow down. Pick a breathing pattern below, choose how long, and follow the orb. A few mindful breaths can steady your mind and body.

🌿 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

  1. Name 5 things you can see around you.
  2. 4 things you can feel (feet on floor, chair, air).
  3. 3 things you can hear, near or far.
  4. 2 things you can smell (or two you like).
  5. 1 thing you can taste — then take a slow breath.
